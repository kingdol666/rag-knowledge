\footnotesize
\emph{Feature definitions:}
\textbf{Domain KBs} --- hierarchical domain-specific knowledge bases with
content-based attribution (A0--A9 pipeline).
\textbf{Auto Archive} --- automatic KB assignment via decision tree, not filename/title.
\textbf{Content Verify} --- per-candidate content scoring (0--8 scale) before inclusion.
\textbf{Knowledge Graph} --- Neo4j-based document relationship graph.
\textbf{Two-Stage} --- BM25 broad recall + vector fine ranking with fusion.
\textbf{Content Scoring} --- confidence tiering (P0/P1/P2) based on verification scores.
\textbf{Exper.\ Cycle} --- structured experience as first-class KB objects (E0--E12).
\textbf{Agent Native} --- MCP tools for full lifecycle (76 tools, 14 skills).

\emph{Key differentiators:}
QDCVR is the only system with (1) content-based KB attribution that prevents
cross-domain false positives at ingestion time, (2) multi-step content verification
that eliminates residual false positives at retrieval time, and (3) an experience
lifecycle enabling structured accumulation of operational knowledge.
