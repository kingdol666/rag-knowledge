\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Packed-context and self-assessing RAG.} Dense retrievers feed top-$k$ chunks to the
generator~\cite{rag-lewis}: provenance is lost at pack time and failed
recall is invisible, whereas \sys{} keeps retrieval and reading as
separate, recorded steps. Self-RAG trains
reflection tokens~\cite{selfrag} and Corrective RAG scores retrieved
documents with a lightweight task-trained evaluator~\cite{crag}; \sys{}'s
judgment layer instead reads full candidate content with a general
locally hosted engine, and its unavailability stops the pipeline rather
than degrading it.

\textbf{Hierarchy, graphs, and abstention.} RAPTOR recurses summaries
into a tree index~\cite{raptor}; DeepRead navigates document
hierarchy~\cite{deepread}; GraphRAG derives community
graphs~\cite{graphrag}. These structures serve retrieval, but the corpus
remains given; in \sys{} the organization loop creates the hierarchy,
and one identifier set carries placement, reads, and citations. \sys{}
also makes abstention structural --- a rung with no plausible candidate
is a terminal, reportable state (measured in
Section~\ref{sec:eval}) --- complementing generator-side calibration
and selective answering~\cite{kamath}.
