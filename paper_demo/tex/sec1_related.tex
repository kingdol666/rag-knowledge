\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Evidence-aware RAG.} RAG couples retrieval with generation
over an external corpus~\cite{rag-lewis}. Self-RAG uses reflection
signals to control retrieval and generation~\cite{selfrag}; Corrective
RAG evaluates retrieved documents before generation~\cite{crag}. Our
system uses an explicit evidence gate, but its contribution is the
inspectable workflow around that gate: candidate retrieval, segment
judgment, addressed reading, and a recorded abstention path.

\textbf{Structured retrieval and knowledge systems.} RAPTOR builds a
hierarchical summary index~\cite{raptor}; DeepRead navigates document
structure~\cite{deepread}; GraphRAG and LightRAG use graph structure
for retrieval and synthesis~\cite{graphrag,lightrag}. These systems
provide relevant retrieval designs. This demo focuses on the adjacent
system problem of keeping collection management and agent retrieval
connected through persistent document addresses, while exposing
content-based ingest checks, multiple retrieval lanes, and source-level
inspection. We make no claim of retrieval-accuracy superiority over
these systems.
