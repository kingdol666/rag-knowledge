\section{Conclusion}
\sys{} is a deployable knowledge-base management platform that organizes documents
by content and verifies every retrieval by reading it. Each query runs one protocol
--- vector-first recall, an interpretable $0$--$8$ content gate, a librarian
fallback, and an honest not-found contract --- and leaves an auditable trace from
candidates to verdict. At the booth: bring a PDF, watch it classified, ask a
question, inspect the score and the citation, and see a genuine not-found. Code and
run artifacts: \url{https://github.com/kingdol666/rag-knowledge}; demo video:
\url{https://github.com/kingdol666/rag-knowledge/blob/master/paper_demo/video/qdcvr-demo.mp4}.

\section*{Data Availability}
All benchmark artifacts are JSON files under the repository's
\texttt{benchmark-suite} results directory: the per-paper parse survey and
ingestion report, the classification decisions with their rationales, the
10-question retrieval regression, per-question gate scores and five-section
answers, the three answering tracks, the three out-of-corpus probe reports, and the
timing logs. The 50-paper corpus is fetched by a reproducible script from the arXiv
identifiers listed in a manifest; every number in this paper traces to one of these
artifacts.
