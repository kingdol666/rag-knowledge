\section{Interactive Demonstration}
\label{sec:demo}

The demonstration targets research administrators and agent developers;
its scenarios trace Section~\ref{sec:intro}'s three decisions in order.
A prepared instance, built by the ingest loop of Section~\ref{sec:system},
holds 100 papers in five bases; attendees inspect documents and drive an
agent with the packaged skills. A live answer takes
from about fifteen seconds to about three minutes, so presenters replay saved traces
alongside live queries; the video walks through these scenarios in the
console, and the repository README's quick start reproduces the instance.

\paragraph{1. Organize a document.}
An attendee selects a PDF from a prepared pool and asks the agent to parse
and place it. The trace records the parse, the proposed base, the tags, and
the indexing checks; the attendee compares parsed text against the routing
before accepting.

\paragraph{2. Inspect an answer's evidence.}
The attendee asks which three ontologies subdivide the Gene Ontology. In
the saved trace (BQ04 in Section~\ref{sec:eval}),
the vector-first search ranks the primer's part~1 of~2 first, at 0.75
(0.66 cross-base); a two-stage pass confirms it, and the agent answers
with a verbatim quotation whose source block names base, part, and
section (``2.~What is the Gene Ontology'') --- score, reading, and
quoted evidence are distinct records; the attendee can open the cited part
in the console while the kb\_* tools read it at its line offset.
Figure~\ref{fig:threeway} compares
the three modes and adds a measured dense-mode abstention (BQ06).

\paragraph{3. Examine a failure report.}
The attendee asks for training epochs in the fictitious paper
\emph{Spectral Tuning for Low-Resource Odor Recognition}. The scripted
report pairs collected evidence with the gate's judgment: BERT's own epoch
recipe scores 1/8, and the skill returns a scoped not-found report instead
of BERT's recipe; the census covers five bases and 322 stored parts, and
bounds only this collection.
