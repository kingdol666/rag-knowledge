\section{Demonstration}
\label{sec:demo}

\paragraph{Demo setup.}
The demo targets IR practitioners and research-data administrators evaluating
alternatives to folder trees, and agent builders wanting auditable retrieval
tools. The live instance holds the 5
category bases with the 50 real papers
(3.8\,M characters) plus three reproduction indices used for comparison (one
underlies Track~C).
Attendees interact through the web console or any MCP-capable agent harness
driving the same 41 \texttt{kb\_*} tools --- one protocol, two surfaces. Attendees may bring their own PDFs and watch them parsed, classified,
and tagged on the spot; all scenarios run on one instance, no state reset.

\paragraph{Scenario 1: from upload to answerable.}
The attendee drops a PDF into the console upload panel. \sys{} parses it into
structured Markdown, splits it into parts that each carry their
section-path header, and files it into the recommended category base, tags
propagated to every part --- no dialog asks the attendee to pick a folder,
schema, or routing rule. The console lists the five
content-classified bases (72, 43, 32, 12, 6 indexed documents) beside the
chunk-built reproduction indices. Point
proven: the administrator writes no rules, and re-filing is logged, not
silent.

\paragraph{Scenario 2: gate-adjudicated answering.}
The attendee asks, ``What defines NISQ technology and what is its central
limitation?'' The console shows Phase~1 recalling from all five bases, the NISQ
paper ranking first at $0.71$ similarity, and
the hit card naming the owning base, document, and part (\texttt{part 2 of 3}).
The gate reads that part, finds the defining sentence plus the
no-error-correction limitation, and scores it 7/8 (an execution-LLM
self-assessment, Section~\ref{sec:eval}) --- a fast exit. Point
proven: the answer carries a readable score and a resolvable citation, not
a bare similarity value.

\paragraph{Scenario 3: the gate discriminates, and failure is honest.}
(a)~\emph{Rescue.} For the tumor-therapy question the initial top chunk is a
header-only part fragment; the gate scores it 5/8, the librarian fallback
re-searches and locates the abstract and equilibrium-analysis sections at 6/8
--- named steps in the trace, not a silent retry. Two of the ten
questions take this path.
(b)~\emph{Honest not-found.} The attendee asks about a paper that does not exist:
``What did `Spectral Tuning for Low-Resource Odor Recognition' report about training
epochs?'' The trap is real --- the top candidate is the BERT paper, which genuinely
contains training-epoch strings a similarity-ranked pipeline would quote.
The gate scores it 1/8 and the shelf scan confirms the paper is absent, so the
system returns a not-found report naming its search scope, the near miss, and why
it does not answer. Three such probes ran (1/8, 1/8, 0/8); attendees are
invited to pose their own. Point proven:
``I don't know'' is a protocol-level, per-query recorded output.
