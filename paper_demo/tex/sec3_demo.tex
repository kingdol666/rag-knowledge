\section{Interactive Demonstration}
\label{sec:demo}

The demonstration targets research administrators and agent developers;
its scenarios trace the platform loop in order --- organize, retrieve
through the ladder, fail honestly. A prepared instance, built by the
ingest loop of Section~\ref{sec:system}, holds 100 papers in five bases
(ingest audit: Table~\ref{tab:pipeline}); attendees inspect documents and
drive an agent with the packaged skills over the kb\_* tools. A live
answer takes from about twenty seconds (a scoped not-found) to about two
minutes (a graded multi-search answer), so presenters replay saved traces
alongside live queries; the video walks these scenarios in the console,
and the repository README's quick start reproduces the instance.

\paragraph{1. Organize a document.}
An attendee selects a PDF from a prepared pool and asks the agent to parse
and place it. The trace records the parse, the proposed base, the tags,
and the indexing checks; the attendee compares parsed text against the
routing before accepting.

\paragraph{2. Watch the ladder retrieve.}
The attendee asks which three ontologies subdivide the Gene Ontology. In
the saved trace (BQ04 in Section~\ref{sec:eval}), the fast lane recalls
the primer with a vector search plus a BM25-style cross-check, reads the
part at its line offset, and the agent answers with a verbatim quotation
whose source block names base, document, part, and section (``2.~What is
the Gene Ontology?''). Score, reading, and quoted evidence are distinct
records; the attendee can open the cited part in the console while the
kb\_* tools read it at its line offset. Figure~\ref{fig:threeway}
compares the three modes on this question and adds a graded-reading
contrast on a second question.

\paragraph{3. Examine a failure report.}
Next, the attendee asks for training epochs in the fictitious paper
\emph{Spectral Tuning for Low-Resource Odor Recognition}. In 22 seconds
the skill returns a conclusion-first not-found report backed by its
searched-facts list: two-stage recall (20 candidates, none on topic),
vector search (10, none), metadata search (weak title-token matches
only), and a per-shelf catalog enumeration (no odor-recognition
documents at all). BERT epoch recipes appear among the retrieved
near-misses and are correctly not substituted; the report states the
remedy --- ingest the paper --- and bounds its claim to this collection.
