\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented an interactive knowledge-base platform that links document
organization to agent retrieval through persistent addresses at the
base, document, part, and section levels. Its engineering contribution is
keeping file-backed content, metadata, and derived indexes inspectable
across ingestion and search. For CIKM's data-management and information
retrieval audience, the expected impact is operational: attendees can follow
a document through ingest checks, inspect evidence behind an answer, and see
whether a not-found result came from missing content or a failed retrieval
path. This is timely for practitioners connecting agent workflows to shared
corpora because the same addresses expose how curation, retrieval, and citation
fit together.
