\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\sys{} demonstrates that a shared paper collection can be operated and
queried through one address space: the identifiers the ingest loop
creates are the ones cited answers carry, and
the librarian fallback routes a failed gate to re-assessment, an
attributed backstop, or a scoped not-found report. The demo scenarios walk
this loop; the monitored run adds operational evidence: grounding and
verification travel together only for the platform's gated reading route
(9/10 each), at roughly three times the bare agent's cost. The
instance --- 100 papers, five bases, 322 stored
parts --- was built and audited by the platform's own skills; every
measured-run number re-derives from saved artifacts. Future
work adds graph navigation as a second route and richer use of recorded
blind spots.
