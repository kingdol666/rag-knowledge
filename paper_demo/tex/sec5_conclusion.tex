\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\sys{} demonstrates that a shared paper collection can be operated and
queried through one address space: the identifiers the ingest loop
creates are the ones cited answers carry, and
the librarian fallback routes a failed gate to re-search, an
attributed backstop, or a scoped not-found report. The demo scenarios walk
this loop; the monitored run adds operational evidence: the platform's gated reading route alone yields answers that are both
highly grounded and highly verified (9/10 each; one run), at roughly
three times the bare agent's cost. The
100-paper, five-base, 322-part instance was built and audited by the
platform's own skills; every measured-run number re-derives from saved
artifacts. Future work adds graph navigation as a second
route and richer use of recorded blind spots.
