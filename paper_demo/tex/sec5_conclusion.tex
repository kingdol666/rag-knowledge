\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\sys{} demonstrates that a shared paper collection can be organized and
queried through one address space: the identifiers the organization loop
creates are the ones cited answers carry, and retrieval runs as a layered
ladder --- catalog, descriptions, wide recall --- whose acceptance step is
a fail-closed engine judgment over actual candidate content. The
monitored run adds operational evidence: the platform's graded-reading
route yields grounded, keyword-verified answers in ten of ten cases (one
run) at about the bare agent's per-question cost; the three retrieval
lanes stay consistent across 27 runs; and abstention behaves as
designed. The 100-paper, five-base, 322-part instance was built and
audited by the platform's own skills; every measured number re-derives
from saved artifacts. Future work adds graph navigation as a second
route and richer use of recorded blind spots.
