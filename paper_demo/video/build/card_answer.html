BQ02 — What defines NISQ technology and what is its central limitation?

content gate 7/8 · fast exit

Answer
NISQ stands for “Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum”. Its central limitation: these devices run without quantum error correction — the overhead of QEC is what keeps near-term devices limited to using noisy qubits directly.
