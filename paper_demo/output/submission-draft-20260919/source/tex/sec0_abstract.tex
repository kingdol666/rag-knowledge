\begin{abstract}
Research administrators need to organize document collections and inspect the
evidence behind answers drawn from them. We present \sys{}, an agent-operated
platform that connects content-guided knowledge organization with inspectable
retrieval. Packaged agent skills call tool APIs to parse documents, route them
among existing category bases, maintain tags, and read candidate evidence.
The retrieval protocol combines vector recall, a prompted content-gate
self-assessment, additional search and reading, and explicit not-found reports.
Gate scores expose the agent's judgment; they do not guarantee semantic
correctness. The demonstration lets attendees follow document placement,
inspect retrieved passages and source references, and examine failure reports.
Saved operational traces cover 50 papers in five bases and 165 stored documents
and parts. A separate ten-question snippet regression passes nine cases, and
three scripted out-of-corpus examples illustrate agent-authored not-found
reports. These operational
spot checks provide concrete cases for examining the workflow, its recorded
decisions, and the limits of available evidence. Demo video:
\url{https://github.com/kingdol666/rag-knowledge/blob/master/paper_demo/video/qdcvr-demo.mp4}.
\end{abstract}
