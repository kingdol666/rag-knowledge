## Official submission requirements
- ACM `sigconf`, double-column, non-anonymous (single-blind). Papers outside the format/length requirements may be desk-rejected.
- At most FOUR pages including body, appendices, acknowledgments. Only GenAI Usage Disclosure and references are excluded from this count. Artifact availability statements are body, not a loophole inside the disclosure.
- The paper must explain what will be demonstrated and how the demo contributes to the relevant area.
- At least one author MUST volunteer to review; without a named reviewer the submission is desk-rejected. The retrieved Demo CFP specifies nomination but does not state a two-paper qualification threshold; do not import another track's criteria. Authors should follow any additional actual submission-system instructions.
- A three-minute demonstration video is requested, with its URL in the paper (e.g., YouTube or Dropbox). The CFP does not require the link to be in the abstract; this draft puts it there for visibility. The locally measured existing video is 176.94 seconds (~2 min 57 sec), close to the requested duration; its scientific claims still need synchronization with the revised manuscript. A direct primary-source HTML snapshot is stored as official-cfp-snapshot.txt.
- The rubric evaluates technical novelty/development challenges, impact and relevance, and asks authors to specify audience, functionality and interaction scenarios. This draft treats those as scientific exposition requirements rather than marketing claims.
- Submission portal: https://easychair.org/my/conference?conf=cikm26
- Date page and Demo CFP agree: abstract May 30, 2026; full submission June 6, 2026; notification August 7, 2026; camera-ready August 23, 2026. AoE deadlines. Main conference November 9–11, 2026, Rome; tutorials November 7 and workshops November 8 per the official Important Dates page.
- As of September 19, 2026 all three deadlines have passed. A new regular submission cannot be represented as currently eligible. Ask the authors whether this is already-submitted/accepted work, an authorized revision, or a future-venue draft.

## Editorial choices, not venue mandates
- Three clear figures with editable vector text, explanatory captions and ACM Description fields.
- Proposed minimum figure label ~7pt at full 178mm double-column width (1000-unit canvas with min14-unit type). Not an official CIKM minimum.
- No manual margin changes, reduced body fonts, negative-spacing squeezing, or arbitrary page-number tricks.
- Reference and disclosure spillover is permitted; all scientific prose and artifact availability remain in body pages1–4.
