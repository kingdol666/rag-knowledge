\section{Conclusion}
\sys{} links knowledge-base management tools with an agent-executed workflow
for reading evidence, assessing relevance, and reporting search failure.

not-found behavior. The saved cases illustrate operation rather than
establish general effectiveness; compliance with the workflow and complete
run provenance remain important validation requirements.
