Conclusion
Qdcvr links knowledge-base management tools with an agent-
executed workflow for reading evidence, assessing relevance, and
reporting search failure. The demonstration exposes document
placement, source inspection, and not-found behavior. The saved
cases illustrate operation rather than establish general effective-
ness; compliance with the workflow and complete run provenance
remain important validation requirements.
Artifacts. Code, corpus-fetch scripts, and saved traces are avail-
able at https://github.com/kingdol666/rag-knowledge. The video
is linked in the abstract. Reproduction requires configured docu-
ment services and an agent harness/model; stored answers are not
independent ground-truth judgments.
