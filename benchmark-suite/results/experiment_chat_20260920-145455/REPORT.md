## Correction appendix (2026-09-21, post-audit)

The Correctness/Monitor tables above were drafted before a trace-level audit
(`audit_paper_numbers.json`, regenerated by
`experiments/audit_paper_numbers.py`). Three statements are corrected:

1. **Track A did record one genuine abstention.** `track_a_BQ05.json` is an
   honest not-found report, not a 10/10-correct answer: every `kb_*` call in
   that session hit the experiment harness's read-only allowlist denial
   ("experiment read-only", 7 denials) and the agent refused to answer from
   parametric memory. Track A outcomes: **9/10 grounded answers + 1 honest
   not-found**.
2. **C/BQ06's abstention cause was the same allowlist denial, not dense
   retrieval insufficiency.** Its two `kb_search_vector` calls were denied
   before execution (plugin-alias tool namespace), so this run provides no
   evidence either way about whether dense retrieval would surface the
   precipitation-efficiency passage. (Earlier runs' retrieval-insufficiency
   findings are separate artifacts.)
3. **Track A averaged 10.2 tool calls per answer (102 total), not 9.7.**

Denial census across the 30 runs: 5 runs issued ≥1 denied call (A: BQ03/04/05/09,
C: BQ06). In three of them (A: BQ03/04/09) the agent recovered by switching to
the allowlisted `mcp__kb-mcp__*` namespace; in two (A: BQ05, C: BQ06) it ended
in an honest not-found report. Use `audit_paper_numbers.json` as the
authoritative aggregate for paper numbers.
