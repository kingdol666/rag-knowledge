**Q:** Which three independent ontologies subdivide the Gene Ontology, and what biological aspects do they represent?

The Gene Ontology is subdivided into three distinct, independent ontologies that represent different biological aspects: (1) Molecular Function (MF) — the molecular activities of gene products, (2) Biological Process (BP) — the biological programs/goals the gene products participate in, and (3) Cellular Component (CC) — the cellular locations where the gene products act. The three ontologies are non-redundant but share a common space of identifiers and a well-specified syntax.
