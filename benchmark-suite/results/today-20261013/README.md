## Reporting rule

A three-slide presentation in `review/deck-benchmark-20261013/` is a synthesis aid, not the source of record; its shorthand “a2 answered 8/8” conflicts with two raw `KB_TOOLS_NOT_READY` rows. The paper uses the raw/summary evidence above and reports a2’s valid citation result as 6/8, with 0.75 over eight as the observed lower bound. The display does not promote its 8/8 shorthand.

Historical results are not mixed into the revised experimental claims. Earlier runs remain out of the paper’s result set; archival/cleanup status is recorded separately.
