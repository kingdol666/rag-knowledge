# Pavle Delibašić

Pavle Delibašić (Serbian Cyrillic: , born 30 November 1978) is a Serbian footballer. He had previously played for Spartak Subotica, Bosnian FK Leotar Trebinje, FK Čukarički Stankom, Chinese Chongqing Lifan and Greek Kallithea F.C., back in Serbia with FK Zemun and with Bulgarian Minyor Pernik and FK Banat Zrenjanin.
