# Lee Jai-jin (musician, born 1979)

Lee Jai-jin (Korean:이재진, born July 13, 1979) is a South Korean singer and dancer. Jai-jin is the sub rapper and main dancer of the South Korean boy group SechsKies, and served as the group's choreographer. SechsKies disbanded in 2000 but made a long-awaited reunion on 'ToToGa 2' special of 'Infinite Challenge' and signed with YG Entertainment in 2016.
