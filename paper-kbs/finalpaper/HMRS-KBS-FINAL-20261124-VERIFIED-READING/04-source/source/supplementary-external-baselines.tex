\noindent\textbf{External-baseline provenance.} The unified batch used one
blind judge and one fresh anonymisation map. All 80 answers, the raw retrieval
records, per-dimension rationales, source-scope declarations, the repeat
subagent batch records (\path{judgments-v4-sub.jsonl}), and earlier
judge-batch summaries are retained in
\path{experiment_data/external_baselines_v3/}. RAPTOR and GraphRAG used the
30-part gold subset; DeepRead's answer-time index covered 23 of its 96
native documents, including the gold papers (gold document present in all
nine recorded contexts), and its parse completed to 96/96 documents after
the answer round; these scope
states are part of the result and are not corrected by post-hoc weighting.
After the twelve-unit cut the retrieval-mode prompts carried per question
on average 9.6~k characters (BM25), 5.7~k (dense), 9.6~k (RRF), 9.6~k
(RAPTOR), 6.6~k (LightRAG) and 9.3~k (GraphRAG); the native DeepRead
agent's recorded evidence units total 6.8~k characters per question
(7.6~k over the nine questions with recorded units). This field does not
aggregate the HMRS agent's multi-turn reading. Table~6 of the main text
reports estimated generator cost as a separate measure.
GraphRAG ran its fast NLP-extraction variant (entity extraction without
language-model calls) and RAPTOR's clustering used a deterministic PCA
replacement for its unstable Gaussian-mixture component; both indexing
configurations are recorded in the experiment adapters of the repository
(\path{review/ext-baselines-20261121/adapters/}).
