\section*{Data availability}
The implementation and benchmark scripts are available at
\url{https://github.com/kingdol666/rag-knowledge}. The archived evaluation
records contain question texts, gold annotations, route and containment
traces, the verifier replay, and the answers, prompts, anonymisation maps
and judgments for the native comparisons. The route
and content traces omit returned passages and reference strings. Source
restrictions limit redistribution of document bodies; the repository
provides a recipe and synthetic sample for constructing a substitute for
the 42-document industrial demonstration corpus.
