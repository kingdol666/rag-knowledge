\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Knowledge management and document acquisition}

Recent work on knowledge-based systems combines language models with curated
knowledge \citep{yang-kbs-llm-2025}. DBpedia publishes structured
information from Wikipedia as linked data \citep{auer-dbpedia-kb-2007}.
Enterprise content management governs the documents through which
organisations retain and share knowledge
\citep{paivarinta-ecm-2005,tyrvaainen-ecm-2006}. Knowledge creation theory
also examines how organisations articulate tacit knowledge
\citep{nonaka-seci-1994}.
Metadata, tagging and human review affect which materials users can find and
trust \citep{park-metadata-quality-2009,golder-tagging-2006,mosqueira-rey-hitl-2022}.
Text categorisation supports placement \citep{sebastiani2002machine}.
Layout-aware parsing and MinerU recover structured content for indexing
\citep{zhong-publaynet-layout-2019,huang-layoutlmv3-docai-2022,mineru}, while
near-duplicate detection and provenance help maintain the collection
\citep{manku-near-dup-2007,missier-prov-2013,cheney-provenance-2009}.
HMRS connects these operations by making the results of acquisition and
placement visible to the workflow that selects evidence.

Agent memory systems such as MemGPT and A-MEM maintain interaction
records and notes \citep{memgpt,amem}. HMRS instead maintains a corpus of
source documents and the descriptions, ownership and indices used to find
them. Knowledge organisation standards such as the Simple Knowledge
Organization System (SKOS) \citep{skos} and
Dublin Core \citep{dcmi-terms} give conventions for vocabularies and
descriptive metadata; at ingestion, the HMRS workflow derives its
descriptions and tags from document content.

Gallofr\'e Oca\~na and Opdahl \citep{ocaa2023software} describe how to
integrate AI and knowledge bases in a newsroom platform. Related systems
ground dialogue in a graph, build open-domain question answering for
low-resource languages, or support retrieval by bank supervisors
\citep{yang2024building,budur2024building,aarab2024system}. Large language
models (LLMs) can also populate domain ontologies \citep{ciatto2025large}.
HMRS applies this systems perspective to changing document collections.
Its focus is the managed state a retrieval workflow reads and an operator
can inspect after a lifecycle operation.

RAG uses retrieved passages to inform generation
\citep{rag-lewis,gao-rag-survey-2023}. Dense
retrieval ranks passages in a learned representation space \citep{dpr}; BM25
uses lexical signals \citep{bm25}. Enterprise architectures combine semantic
retrieval with vector storage and LLMs \citep{ghali-kbs-retrieval-2025}.
Hybrid search combines ranking signals, for example with reciprocal-rank
fusion (RRF) \citep{rrf-cormack}, and BGE-M3 provides multilingual and
multi-granularity embeddings \citep{bge-m3}. Neural rerankers score
query--passage pairs with cross-encoders \citep{nogueira-monobert-2019} or
late interaction \citep{khattab-colbert-2020}. Generators can combine
information across retrieved passages, as in Fusion-in-Decoder
\citep{izacard-grave}. HMRS makes a separate decision before generation:
a server-side score determines which passages enter the pack without
requiring the caller to use that score to order documents.

Structured retrieval changes the units a ranker searches.
GraphRAG builds entity graphs and community
summaries \citep{graphrag}. LightRAG searches an incrementally updated
graph at two retrieval levels \citep{lightrag}, and RAPTOR recursively
groups text into a cluster tree \citep{raptor}. RMR-SST represents multimodal engineering
documents as semantic trees \citep{li-kbs-rmrsst-2026}. Dense X Retrieval
indexes propositions \citep{chen2024dense}, while TEIE-RAG combines
hierarchical semantic chunking with source highlighting for engineering
information extraction \citep{shen-kbs-teierag-2026}.
These hierarchies are \emph{derived}---computed from content at index
time, as is DeepRead's per-document section structure---whereas HMRS
collections are \emph{declared}: operator-maintained membership and
descriptions that scope search before ranking. The two kinds are
orthogonal and could be composed.
Table~\ref{tab:related-comparison} locates HMRS among these designs: it
organises access around maintained collection membership and document
structure.

Index maintenance complements this hierarchy by keeping passages reachable
after document changes \citep{xu2023spfresh}. Content-level freshness is a
separate measurement. FreshQA tests questions whose answers change over
time \citep{vu-freshqa-2024}. The HMRS campaigns hold the corpus fixed
within each record.

Distributed IR treats collection selection and result merging as its two
subproblems \citep{callan1995searching,si2003redde,shokouhi-federated-2011}.
Like CORI and ReDDE, HMRS selects collections from representations of
their contents. In HMRS, operators can inspect and maintain collection
descriptions, and the catalogue-first policy uses a second set of
descriptions on each shelf to choose documents. Tool-using agents can
choose and iteratively refine what they retrieve
\citep{react-yao,toolformer}, interleaving retrieval with reasoning
\citep{ircot} or adapting the retrieval strategy to question complexity
\citep{adaptive-rag}. MCP provides standard
interfaces to these tools \citep{mcp}. Modular RAG studies how these
workflows combine retrieval components \citep{modular-rag}. Agentic RAG
surveys systematise agent-planned retrieval and tool use
\citep{singh-agentic-rag-2025}, the setting of the access policies evaluated
here. DeepRead illustrates structure-aware agent access by indexing paragraphs with section
coordinates and reading selected sections \citep{deepread}. HMRS gives
an agent a catalogue and shelf to determine which documents to open, then
exposes document and passage access through the same tool layer.

In engineering design, structured facts extracted from patents support RAG
\citep{siddharth2024retrieval}. Other approaches change which evidence a
query can reach through soft-prompt tuning, semantic query expansion and
stateful graph exploration
\citep{peng2024soft,massai2023evaluatio,tao2025fine}. HMRS exposes the document
state on which collection and passage selection depend.

\subsection{Verification, correction and abstention}

Self-RAG and CRAG assess evidence or correct retrieval during generation
\citep{selfrag,crag}; FLARE triggers retrieval as more evidence is needed
\citep{flare}. Work on selective prediction under domain shift and
uncertainty reporting examines when to withhold an answer
\citep{kamath,feng-abstain,yin-knowwhat}. Citation studies examine whether
references support the statements in the answer
\citep{liu-verifiability,gao-citations}.
EPRAG diagnoses evidence states to choose among response, clarification and
abstention in enterprise retrieval \citep{kinalioglu-kbs-eprag-2026}.
HMRS provides an optional server-side passage-admission scorer. The gate
fetches candidate text, scores passages and returns surviving text with
provenance, so an agent can inspect the source material behind the result.
The score does not itself establish claim-level support.

Judge studies report preference bias, variable agreement and ranking
instability, including position and verbosity effects
\citep{zheng-llmjudge,sun-urara,panickssery-selfpref,wang-llmnotfair}.
Logic-grounded metamorphic testing checks whether model decisions remain
consistent under equivalent input transformations \citep{zhou2026lgmt}.
The HMRS evaluation examines passage admission, candidate ordering and
answer reporting separately. The blind answer-quality comparison in
Section~\ref{sec:e10} reports the judge's protocol and limitations
alongside its scores.

\subsection{Research gap}
\label{sec:gap}

RMR-SST and TEIE-RAG structure document content for retrieval, EPRAG
selects a response action once candidate evidence is available
\citep{li-kbs-rmrsst-2026,shen-kbs-teierag-2026,kinalioglu-kbs-eprag-2026},
and enterprise content management governs placement and description over
time \citep{paivarinta-ecm-2005,park-metadata-quality-2009}. These lines of
work address different points in the path from organising documents to
answering a question. HMRS connects two of those points: it exposes
operator-maintained membership and descriptions to the retrieval agent,
and lets operators check after selected lifecycle changes whether the
index still reaches the document. Table~\ref{tab:related-comparison}
locates this contribution alongside the closest systems.
