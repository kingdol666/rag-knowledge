\section*{Supplementary Material}
}

\noindent\textbf{Reading the supplementary material.} The material is
grouped by evidence type, and each result retains the corpus and unit
reported in the main text. Figure~S1 distinguishes index and cache failures
that produce similar search symptoms. Figures~S2--S3 report answer-marker and out-of-corpus probe
observations. Figure~S4 quantifies the two verifier-score replays, and Figure~S5
makes one logged query inspectable. Figure~S6 retains two evidence-path cases,
and Figure~S7 shows the two-grid marker matrix condensed out of the main text.
Table~S9 indexes the management checks, Table~S10 summarises
the retrieval and answer designs, and Table~S11 positions representative
research strands. Tables~S12--S13 give arm counts and paired contrasts for
E4. The reruns reuse the same in-domain questions on later corpus states.
Tables~S14--S16 retain the
question-level answer proxies, aggregate answer and runtime observations,
and the DeepSeek-R1 answer-case comparison. For the 72 in-domain questions,
a post-hoc check lowercased each question and its nonempty annotated target,
then tested exact substring containment without other normalisation. The
29 overlapping questions had frozen containment of 16/29 for A0 and 21/29
for A0h; the other 43 had 21/43 and 31/43, respectively. These non-random
subsets are descriptive, not a causal test, and other lexical cues may remain
in the non-overlap group. Table~S17 records cohort provenance for selected campaigns; Table~S18 reports question-level scores from the
earlier judge pass of E2. Tables~S19--S20 compare judge batches and give the
unified question-level scores; Table~S21 gives the earlier four-arm aggregate
and its paired contrasts. Table~S22 reports the separate seven-comparison
Holm family for the unified batch. The complete 80-answer transcript is
provided as the companion \path{external-baselines-qa.pdf} and as raw JSONL.
The companion evidence index describes what is and is not available. Each
trace CSV packages 76 question IDs per complete arm; the in-domain analyses
exclude the four out-of-domain probes R1--R4.
