\section{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\noindent\textbf{Data and scope.} Developers authored or expanded the
questions and gold annotations for local demonstration and benchmark
collections. Same-question reruns test whether the direction persists as
corpus and service state change, not whether it transfers to new domains.
Storage reconciliation was sampled; out-of-domain probes describe individual
responses, not a population failure rate.

\noindent\textbf{Measurement.} A literal target string can occur in a sibling
document. In a post-hoc split by case-insensitive question--target overlap,
frozen A0 and A0h scored 16/29 and 21/29 on overlapping questions and
21/43 and 31/43 on the remainder. This split does not measure other lexical
cues. E7 checks identifiers, keywords and citation markers with one model
and one run per arm. Claim support requires resolving the cited passages and
checking them against the claims.

\noindent\textbf{Configuration contrasts.} Read-all scans the corpus
mechanically. The evidence-pack arms vary candidate construction, reading
scope and assembly. A0, A1b and A3 may use the gold domain for reference
resolution; A3 may fall back to descriptions or search outside the evaluated
collections. These paths were not counted, and E4 does not isolate
individual components.

\noindent\textbf{Answer-quality protocol.} E2 reuses ten development and
post-repair questions. Its archived tool-name logs contain no passages or
source coordinates. The judge could not resolve baseline \texttt{[S\#]}
markers; system names were hidden, but language, length and citation style
remained visible. Earlier passes yielded lower baseline scores and some
reversals: in the one-answer-per-call pass, dense retrieval scored 5.00
against HMRS's 3.75 on BQ07. Judge--human and judge--judge agreement were
not measured.

\noindent\textbf{External-system comparison.} The configurations in
Section~\ref{sec:e10} did not share an answer-time corpus. In particular,
DeepRead's full parse finished after the answer round; the smaller indexed
search space may favour it. Its top-level generator field and native
provenance note also disagree. The comparison is not a matched-corpus
leaderboard.

\noindent\textbf{Statistical and operational validity.} The paired tests are
exploratory. The nominal binomial Wilson intervals do not capture variation
across runs. Supplementary Tables~S18--S22 show sensitivity to judge pass
and the comparison family. Treating candidates or spans from one query as
independent can understate uncertainty. E6 replays a historical wrapper;
some earlier reference-mode and judge inputs and code hashes are missing.
Lifecycle and concurrency checks cover one installation. Competing lifecycle
writes, experience approval, multi-host reliability and crash recovery
remain open.
