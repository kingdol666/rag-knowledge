Dear Editors of Knowledge-Based Systems,

We submit our manuscript "HMRS: A hierarchical management-and-retrieval
system for tiered knowledge document organisation and evidence-traced
access" for consideration as a research paper.

Retrieval-augmented pipelines usually assume that a corpus is already
indexed and governed. In practice, parsed documents drift: placements
change, indexes go stale, and answers lose the link to their sources. Our
manuscript studies the missing half — management — as a first-class system
concern. HMRS manages collections, documents and segments as explicit
objects and serves catalogue-first access through one auditable state,
with an optional evidence gate that admits scored passages without
claiming entailment.

We believe the work fits Knowledge-Based Systems because it is a
knowledge-document management system contribution: it reports the design,
an MCP-based implementation, and a bounded, artifact-anchored evaluation
rather than a new ranking algorithm. Three evidence lines carry the
claims. First, management and lifecycle audits (three-source consistency,
rename and re-index failure modes, cross-language assignment) establish
that the state is inspectable and repairable, including where it fails.
Second, on a 72-question routing corpus, the tiered chunk-window
configuration held the highest exact gold-string containment in three
recorded executions (52/72 frozen; 51/72 in a reproduction and a
same-question follow-up), with paired contrasts of 15-0 and 12-0 against
the tiered baseline; the navigation-free comparison remains statistically
unresolved and is reported as such. Third, at the answer layer, gold
identifiers appeared in every recorded cell while document-style citation
markers separated document-level organisation from a flat chunk replica
(9/10 versus 2/10; 10/10 after repair) — a lexical contrast we explicitly
do not read as semantic quality. A same-pool replay additionally shows why
verifier scores must not be used for ordering.

All statements are traceable to frozen archives; limitations, exploratory
statistics and non-independent follow-up are disclosed in the manuscript.
The work is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors
have approved the submission. We declare no competing interests; funding
and data availability are stated in the manuscript. Generative-AI use for
experiment execution, tabulation, language editing and initial figure
design is declared before the references.

Thank you for your consideration.
