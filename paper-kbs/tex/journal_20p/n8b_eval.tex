\subsection{E3: Operational knowledge and automated distillation}
\label{sec:e3}

The experience store holds operational lessons as first-class retrievable
documents---addressed, indexed and searched as an ordinary document, with a
credibility tier deciding whether it may be shown at all
(Section~\ref{sec:org-experience}). Three case measurements exist: an
extraction campaign over one small knowledge base produced thirteen
candidate drafts
(\path{benchmark-suite/results/experience_smoke.json}:
\texttt{extract\_candidates}~13, \texttt{drafts\_created}~13); one
end-to-end scenario was sedimented into one experience
(\texttt{exp-d6f4d47ae3d6}) that a later global experience search returned
first, at local similarity 0.711 (\path{review/fulltest-20261012}, scenario
A6); and the draft-approval path was never exercised---the draft pool was
empty (\texttt{count}~0), so \path{experience_draft_read} and
\path{experience_draft_approve} could not run
(\path{benchmark-suite/results/SKILL-E2E-REPORT.md}, lines~27 and~103),
the heuristic extraction path having run only in dry-run mode, returning a
dump the quality gate would reject (line~100). One scenario and thirteen
candidates cannot support an effectiveness claim;
scheduled distillation, review and approval at scale, application feedback
and credibility decay went unexercised. What the measurements show is
narrower and sufficient for the design argument: an operational lesson can
travel the document path, with its tier attached---and nothing more.

\subsection{E4: Retrieval quality and the role of the verdict}
\label{sec:e4}
\label{sec:eval-retrieval}

Nine configurations, defined here so the table reads on its own. Seven were
measured through their own tool-layer entry points: \emph{BM25} via the
keyword search tool; \emph{Dense} via \texttt{kb\_search\_vector} with
BGE-M3 \cite{bge-m3}; \emph{RRF} as reciprocal-rank fusion at $k=60$ of the
two \cite{rrf-cormack}; \emph{Graph} via \texttt{kb\_graph\_search}, a
node-name and path match over the document graph; \emph{Two-stage} via
\texttt{kb\_search\_two\_stage} (Section~\ref{sec:twostage}); and the hybrid
lane via \texttt{kb\_hybrid\_search} with the gate off (\emph{Fusion-only})
and on (\emph{QDCVR-Hybrid}), in the shipped tool layer a server-side
composition of the vector, catalog and judging tools
(\texttt{kb\_hybrid\_search} was an earlier single-call wrapper, no longer
registered). Two rows are derived offline by replaying the judged hybrid
run's logged twelve-candidate pools and verdict scores:
\emph{Judge-as-filter} keeps survivors in fusion order and \emph{RRF-fuse}
fuses the fusion ranking with the judge-score ranking at $k=60$. All three
see the same candidates and scores, so the comparison is an attribution,
not a benchmark.

\textbf{On this corpus, lexical retrieval is strong.} BM25 \cite{bm25}
hits Hit@5 1.00 at about a millisecond per call, reciprocal-rank fusion
\cite{rrf-cormack} matches it, and the two-stage BM25+graph-then-dense
pipeline reaches Recall@5 0.97 (Table~\ref{tab:retrieval}). Dense
retrieval with BGE-M3 \cite{bge-m3} misses one question (Q16), where four
near-duplicate sibling reports crowd the top-5 --- a confuser-density
failure, not a vocabulary one; graph search \cite{graphrag}, limited to
node-name and tag matching, is the weakest standalone signal. Nothing here
is a retrieval advance: three configurations tie at Hit@5 1.00, and the one
using only words leads on MRR.

\textbf{The central result is the verdict's role, not its existence.} The
same judged scores enter three configurations: not used (fusion-only,
Hit@5 0.94), used to re-rank (QDCVR-Hybrid, 0.44), and used as a filter
keeping survivors in fusion order (judge-as-filter, 0.94). On the eight
questions where re-ranking and filtering differ (Q2, Q4, Q5, Q6, Q8, Q9,
Q11, Q12), filtering wins all eight and loses none, with eight further
ties on Hit@5, and it restores MRR to 0.856 (exact binomial sign test,
two-sided $p = 0.008$); the only adverse ranking movement is Q7, where the gold
slips from third to fifth inside the top-5, while Q1, Q10, Q13 and Q15 move
upward. The verdicts were not wrong
about which documents are evidence --- they were wrong as an ordering,
matching the judge-miscalibration pattern reported for model-generated
preferences \cite{zheng-llmjudge,panickssery-selfpref}; the fix is the
placement of the verdict, not its existence or its strength
(Figure~\ref{fig:marginvariants}(b)). The price is
measurable: judging twelve candidates adds well under a second with the
resident worker, the hybrid call's surcharge over fusion-only being the
observed difference between 542\,ms and 62\,ms per call. Two caveats: the
test is post-hoc and descriptive---one of nine configurations on the same
sixteen questions---and the gate is a single locally fine-tuned
checkpoint (Section~\ref{sec:gate}), which every verifier finding in this
paper inherits.

\textbf{A second corpus reproduces the baselines, not the collapse.} Ten
questions over the 79 retrievable papers of the 100-paper arXiv ingestion
(8{,}031 chunks logged; block~(b) of
Table~\ref{tab:retrieval};
\path{paper-kbs/experiments/results/exp10_papers_toollayer.json}) are
answered at Hit@5 0.90 by seven of the eight configurations, graph search
excepted at 0.20. Question BQ03 is missed by all eight: its gold paper is
one of the 21 papers that never became retrievable in that campaign, so the
single shared miss is an acquisition-side loss, not a retrieval failure,
and we report both denominators---0.90 over the ten questions as asked,
1.00 over the nine answerable ones. The judged collapse does not reproduce
here, where the same-family confuser cluster that drives it is absent; the
graph configuration's 0.20 tracks absent relation tagging, the bulk import
having bypassed the tagging path.

\textbf{Thresholds and margins.} All 191 judged candidates from the
sixteen hybrid runs passed the 0.5 acceptance threshold (lowest logged
score 0.589), so at the threshold layer the gate filtered nothing on this
corpus (\texttt{exp1\_retrieval.json}). The margin sweep replays these
logged pairs rather than re-retrieving: 0.15 keeps 16 of the
17 in-pool golds and 0.20 all 17; leave-one-out selection
reaches 0.9375, missing only Q11, which the shipped 0.15 also loses,
while a fixed 0.20 floor reaches 1.00 (\texttt{exp6\_loo\_margin.json};
Figure~\ref{fig:marginvariants}(a) plots the sweep). The shipped floor is an
engineering constant, not a fitted parameter; the calibration that would
justify it has not been run.

\textbf{Rephrasing shows where the fragility sits.} On sixteen paraphrase
questions (\texttt{exp6\_paraphrase.json}) the judged configuration falls
to Hit@5 0.5 (MRR 0.189) while judge-as-filter and fusion-only hold at
0.9375 (MRR 0.833) and dense retrieval stays at 0.9375; BM25 falls from
1.00 to 0.875 (MRR 0.958 to 0.755). Under rewording both the lexical
baseline and the judged re-ranking lose ground while the configuration that
refuses to let the verdict order the list does not --- the gate's weakness
is the ordering it induces, not the verdicts it returns.

\textbf{End to end, the modes differ in checkability.} Ten fixed questions
ran through three modes sharing one harness, one model and one neutral
prompt template, differing only in retrieval affordance: the platform's
read surface, a bare agent with file tools over the exported papers, and
dense retrieval over an 800-character replica index (4{,}528 chunks). All
three answer, the bare agent grounding as many as the platform
(10/10 vs.\ 10/10), but it names files where the platform names checkable
addresses, and dense retrieval cites an address once in ten questions.
Caveats: both dense grounding misses were tool-availability aborts;
word-boundary matching zeroes the bare agent's plural ``cathodes'' on BQ08;
two platform cells re-ran once after a harness timeout; correctness calls
are the authors' trace readings; cache state was uncontrolled.


E4 establishes where the verdict belongs and what it costs there:
verification before answering is not itself new \cite{selfrag,crag}, but
placed as a filter rather than a ranker it is the difference between a
citation and a claim, since the ordering the reader sees is not the
verdict's. E4 establishes no retrieval advance---BM25 reaches Hit@5 1.00 on
this corpus (Figure~\ref{fig:retrieval})---so recall is not the contribution,
and no margin, threshold or paraphrase
variant tested repairs the ordering role of the verdicts, which the failure
analysis of the verifier takes up.
