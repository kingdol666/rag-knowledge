\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

QDCVR studies a knowledge-base workflow in which content informs placement,
a server-side Laya tool checks candidate text when invoked, and an answer
workflow is instructed to report not-found after a completed search finds no
support. These are mechanisms at different layers, not three equivalent
server-enforced guarantees. Storage and consistency checks are mechanically
performed; semantic placement remains an ingest-agent decision; the answer
layer's abstention behavior is evaluated from observed trials rather than
certified by a server-side semantic classifier.

The evaluation supports a bounded set of findings. On the industrial corpus,
lexical retrieval reaches Hit@5 1.00. Replaying the same recorded candidate
pools shows that judge-score re-ranking falls to Hit@5 0.44, whereas the
fusion-order replay among recorded survivors matches fusion-only at 0.94. All
191 recorded scores exceed the 0.5 threshold, and one additional fusion
candidate has no recorded score; the comparison therefore diagnoses a harmful
ordering role, not a benefit from threshold filtering. In the end-to-end
grid, all 30 answer texts contain a gold document identifier. Strict keyword
coverage is 9/10 in each arm---the misses are word-form mismatches---and
alias-aware coverage is 10/10 in each arm; the BQ06 gold key was corrected to
the source paper's reading, and both grades are retained.
Recognized citation-marker hits are 9/10, 7/10 and 2/10 for platform, file
and dense arms. These measures capture identifier presence and lexical/marker
coverage, not semantic support. Acquisition left
21 of 100 submissions unretrievable without a complete failure inventory, and
a selected oversized-catalog path was repaired and retested after a false
refusal.

The contribution is an auditable deployment account and a diagnostic warning
against using this verifier checkpoint as a ranker under the tested conditions,
not a claim of universal retrieval or answer-quality superiority. Next steps
include independently curated questions, repeated agent trials, claim-level
evidence-support evaluation, explicit execution-failure states for incomplete
searches, transport stress tests and operator studies of not-found reports.
