Reading the supplementary material. The material is grouped by evidence type, and
each result retains the corpus and unit reported in the main text. Figure S1 shows the service
boundaries and shared state. Figures S2–S3 report answer-marker and out-of-corpus probe
observations; Figures S4–S5 replay verifier roles and illustrate one logged query. Table S9
indexes the management checks, Table S10 summarises the retrieval and answer designs, and
Table S11 positions representative research strands. Tables S12–S13 give arm counts and paired
contrasts for E5. The reruns reuse the same in-domain questions on later corpus states.
Tables S14–S16 retain the question-level answer proxies, aggregate answer and runtime
observations, and the DeepSeek-R1 answer-case comparison. For the 72 in-domain questions, a
post-hoc check lowercased each question and its nonempty annotated target, then tested exact
substring containment without other normalization. The 29 overlapping questions had frozen
containment of 16/29 for A0 and 21/29 for A0h; the other 43 had 21/43 and 31/43, respectively.
These non-random subsets are descriptive, not a causal test, and other lexical cues may remain in
the non-overlap group. Table S17 records the design and provenance of every campaign named
in the main text. Tables S1–S8 of the companion inventory document (shipped alongside this
supplement) complete the campaign-level evidence index. Each trace CSV packages 76 question
IDs per complete arm. The in-domain analyses exclude the four out-of-domain probes R1–R4.

queries returned natural-language not-found reports and no fabricated citation was observed.
The latter is an observation of instructed agent behaviour, not a server-side guarantee.

Figure S4. Two replays over the logged hybrid pools analysed in the main paper’s verifier
section; both reuse recorded candidate pools and verdict scores rather than retrieving again.
(a) A relative-margin replay: keep the top five plus every candidate within m of the pool’s best
score, m swept 0.05–0.35; the curve counts gold instances kept of the 17 that entered a pool;
dashed rules mark m=0.15 (16 of 17) and m=0.20 (all 17); the mean candidate count rises from
6.4 to 11.9 of the twelve submitted. (b) Verdict roles on identical candidates and identical
scores: fusion order, verdict order and verdict filter; only the role differs, which makes the panel
an attribution rather than a benchmark; the verdict-order role loses the gold, while the filter role
leaves the fusion order intact. Hit@5 is 0.94, 0.44 and 0.94.

Figure S5. Anatomy of one real query (Q2; single logged HMRS-Hybrid call). Each line is
one of the 12 fused candidates; the shaded band is the top-5 window selected for source reading
in the recorded run. (a) In the logged re-ranking, the judge scores five extruder-PLC telemetry
reports 0.887–0.926 and the gold root-cause report 0.846, demoting it from fusion rank 1 to
rank 8 — outside the read window. (b) The same call with the verdicts used only as a filter: all
12 pass, the fusion order stands, and the gold is the first document read. Same pool, same scores,
same verdicts; only the role of the induced ordering differs.
