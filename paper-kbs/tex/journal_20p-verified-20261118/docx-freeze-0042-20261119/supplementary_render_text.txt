Reading the supplementary material. The material is grouped by evidence type, and
each result retains the corpus and unit reported in the main text. Figure S1 shows the service
boundaries and shared state. Figures S2–S3 report answer-marker and out-of-corpus probe
observations; Figures S4–S5 replay verifier roles and illustrate one logged query. Table S9
indexes the management checks, Table S10 summarises the retrieval and answer designs, and
Table S11 positions representative research strands. Tables S12–S13 give arm counts and paired
contrasts for E5. The reruns reuse the same in-domain questions on later corpus states.
Tables S14–S16 retain the question-level answer proxies, aggregate answer and runtime
observations, and the DeepSeek-R1 answer-case comparison. For the 72 in-domain questions, a
post-hoc check lowercased each question and its nonempty annotated target, then tested exact
substring containment without other normalization. The 29 overlapping questions had frozen
containment of 16/29 for A0 and 21/29 for A0h; the other 43 had 21/43 and 31/43, respectively.
These non-random subsets are descriptive, not a causal test, and other lexical cues may remain in
the non-overlap group. Table S17 records the design and provenance of selected campaigns.
Tables S1–S8 of the companion inventory document (shipped alongside this supplement) give
historical campaign-level details. The E5 trace exports contain 76 question IDs per complete
arm. The in-domain analyses exclude the four out-of-domain probes R1–R4.
