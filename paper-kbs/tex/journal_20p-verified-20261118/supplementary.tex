\section*{Supplementary Material}
}

\noindent\textbf{Reading the supplementary material.} The material is
grouped by evidence type, and each result retains the corpus and unit
reported in the main text. Figure~S1 shows the service boundaries and
shared state. Figures~S2--S3 report answer-marker and out-of-corpus probe
observations; Figures~S4--S5 replay verifier roles and illustrate one
logged query. Table~S9 indexes the management checks, Table~S10 summarises
the retrieval and answer designs, and Table~S11 positions representative
research strands. Tables~S12--S13 give arm counts, costs and paired contrasts.
The Main comparison reused the same questions after a service restart.
For the 29 questions whose text includes the complete target string, its
packs matched in 16/29 cases for A0 and 21/29 for A0h. For the remaining
43, the counts were 20/43 and 27/43. This descriptive split does not
control for other lexical cues. Tables~S14--S16 give the answer grids,
the standalone answer check and the case study; Table~S17 records the
campaign boundaries. Figures~S6--S7 show the historical marker and verifier
comparisons. Tables~S1--S8 of the companion inventory document give
historical campaign details. See \path{experiment-source-map.md} for
experiment provenance.
The E5 trace exports contain 76 question IDs per complete arm. The in-domain
analyses exclude the four out-of-domain probes R1--R4.
