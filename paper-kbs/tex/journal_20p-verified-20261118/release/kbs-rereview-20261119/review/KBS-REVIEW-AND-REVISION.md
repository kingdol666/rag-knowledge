# HMRS pre-submission review and response to findings

**Scope.** Five role-separated AI reviews examined the frozen November-evidence draft: journal writing/contribution, methodology, domain accuracy, engineering transfer, and adversarial argument review. The seats committed criteria before receiving the frozen manuscript and did not receive one another's reports. This is an author-side simulation, not journal peer review, a calibrated statistical panel, or an ARS target-binding certification. The manuscript was not sent to an external reviewer. The publisher checklist below was evaluated separately from the scholarly review.

## Editorial assessment of the frozen version: major revision

The traceable management of document state, rather than a new ranking algorithm, is the clearest systems contribution. The evidence supports selected management checks and an exploratory, fixed-question comparison between concrete retrieval configurations. It does not yet establish passage-level relevance, semantic support, or gains that generalise to a new corpus or installation. The frozen draft warrants major revision because the headline byte comparison uses different accounting boundaries, the route comparison does not isolate descriptions, and the paper needed a clearer demonstration of what a state or passage trace lets an operator inspect. The adversarial review found no issue meeting the singleton rejection threshold: the cost claim is repairable by narrowing the interpretation; the existing corpus, response captures and rename observations remain informative within scope.

## Independent publisher checklist

Checked the [current KBS Guide for Authors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/knowledge-based-systems/publish/guide-for-authors) and [Elsevier AI policy](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals) on 2026-11-19. KBS allows double-column LaTeX but requires single-column Word. The Research Article guide says **preferably** no more than 20 double-spaced pages including tables/figures. The double-spaced check remains above that recommendation; judge it from its own PDF, not the journal-layout preview. The guide calls for ≤250 abstract words, 1–7 English keywords and encourages a separate 3–5-bullet Highlights file (≤85 characters each). KBS follows single-anonymized review. Editable source, tables, and stand-alone figures are supplied; the archive is not a submission.

Option C requires a suitable research-data repository and link at submission, or an explanation where sharing is not possible. The attached CSVs reproduce saved-label summaries; they do not independently verify preserved or missing passages. No data deposit, submission or Git push was performed. Confirm author names/affiliations, CRediT, funding, interests, prior dissemination/overlap and the relevant data rights before submission. The current AI policy permits eligible explanatory illustrations and data-derived reproducible visualizations subject to disclosure; verify which tools were used in final images, and disclose where required in the relevant captions as well as the general AI statement. Do not infer those details from concept prototypes.

## What would change the scientific verdict

A single frozen public long-document test with paragraph-level labels, matched candidate and pack budgets, no gold-domain fallback, complete packs and reportable failure status would strengthen the empirical contribution more than further tuning on the same 72 questions. Pair paragraph recall and cost measured at a common boundary with separate, blinded claim-support judgments. That evaluation has not been run in this revision.
