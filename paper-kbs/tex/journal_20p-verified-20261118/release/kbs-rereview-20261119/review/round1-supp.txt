                                                   Supplementary Material

Reading the supplementary material. The material is grouped by evidence type, and each result retains the corpus and unit
reported in the main text. Figure S1 shows the service boundaries and shared state. Figures S2–S3 report answer-marker and
out-of-corpus probe observations; Figures S4–S5 replay verifier roles and illustrate one logged query. Table S9 indexes the man-
agement checks, Table S10 summarises the retrieval and answer designs, and Table S11 positions representative research strands.
Tables S12–S13 give arm counts, costs and paired contrasts. The November 17 rerun reused the same questions on a restarted stack.
For the 29 questions whose text includes the complete target string, the November 17 packs matched in 16/29 cases for A0 and
21/29 for A0h. For the remaining 43, the counts were 20/43 and 27/43. This is a descriptive split, not a control for other lexical
cues. Tables S14–S16 preserve the answer grids and case study; Table S17 records the campaign boundaries. Figures S6–S7 pre-
serve the historical marker and verifier comparisons. Tables S1–S8 of the companion inventory document (shipped alongside this
supplement) give historical campaign-level details. The E5 trace exports contain 76 question IDs per complete arm. The in-domain
analyses exclude the four out-of-domain probes R1–R4.
