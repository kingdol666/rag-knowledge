\label{sec:ladder-opt}
\textbf{Content-layer configurations.} The access comparison varies route
selection, candidates, reading window and judging together. A0 combines
route-card selection with a 40K reference-mode judge; A0f reads full document
windows inline, up to 250K characters; A0h adds the top-eight vector-chunk
windows to A0f with a 60K evidence cap. PD omits catalogue navigation and
judges a per-library top-six document union. A1b merges scoped vector results
from ten evaluation libraries and judges a 40K reference window; A2 uses a
scoped vector union and concatenated snippets without the judge. A3 uses
nominally name-only cards. A1b and A2 are per-library searches, not unrestricted
global searches. Some reference lookups may use the gold domain; their
frequency is unknown. The historical traces do not preserve every prompt or
configuration hash. Consequently these arms are configuration comparisons,
not isolated navigation or gate ablations.

In the frozen 11 November aggregate, A0/A0f/A0h/PD/A1b/A2/A3 contained the
gold string for 37/45/52/46/39/25/33 of 72 questions. A mechanical read-all
scan found 72/72 strings; it reads stored parts and produces no answers. In
the later same-question record, the seven configurations contained the string
for 39/44/51/45/39/25/25 cases, respectively
(Figure~\ref{fig:ladder}). A0h was highest among the completed follow-up arms
at 51/72 (70.8\%). The later read-all record was incomplete, with three gold
rows at the report cutoff; it is not a 72-question comparison. A3 was added
separately and the reason for its change is unresolved. Supplementary Tables
S12--S14 give per-arm results and paired records.

For the frozen aggregate, A0h contained the string when A1b did not for 13
questions and the reverse occurred for none (two-sided exact McNemar
$p=0.00024$). Against PD the discordant count was 7--1 in A0h's favour
($p=0.0703$). In the later record the A0h--A0 contrast was 12--0
($p=0.00049$). These are unadjusted, exploratory paired comparisons of
string containment; they do not establish a single-component effect or an
advantage in semantic answer quality. In particular, A2 differs from A0 in
routing, candidate construction and returned text as well as judging.

A separate frozen 40-question diagnostic returned the gold string in 24/40
BM25 and 19/40 dense cases. Concatenating the top five texts from each list
gave 26/40; the historical label \texttt{F-rrf} denotes text concatenation,
not reciprocal-rank fusion. BM25 uses the local corpus whereas dense search
uses the global index. These results differ in scope and text construction
from the 72-question experiment and should not be compared as a matched
benchmark. Later 40-question records use different conditions.

Among the frozen A0's 35 misses, A0f, A0h and PD contained the string in 8,
15 and 9 cases, respectively; these sets overlap. A post-hoc location search
cannot identify causes because a matching file need not be a candidate read
by the experiment. The mechanical read-all scan recorded 24.3 MB and 253.7 s
per question, whereas A0h recorded 309 KB and 13.8 s; the byte measures can
overlap and differ between procedures, and tool time excludes answer
generation. They are not comparable model-input or end-to-end answer costs.
