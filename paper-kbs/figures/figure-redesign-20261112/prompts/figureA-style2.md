# HMRS Figure A — Style 2 visual direction board

Warm study-style academic figure visual direction board for a Knowledge-Based Systems paper about HMRS, a managed knowledge-document platform. Warm ivory background, deep navy lines, muted teal and sage for services, amber gold for evidence and management decisions, muted violet for persistent state. Use a premium but restrained editorial research-diagram composition: three management layers as a vertical stack, service boundaries as a middle band, persistent files/registries/indices as a grounded foundation, and a separate right-hand narrowing query ladder. Use visual rhythm, generous negative space, subtle paper-like warmth, clean flat geometry, no shadows, no 3D, no icons that imply unsupported features. Exact scientific labels, numbers, ports, arrows, and semantics will be typeset later in native SVG/Matplotlib; do not rely on generated text. No watermark, no fake logos, no invented modules.
