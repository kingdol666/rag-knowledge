# HMRS Figure A — Style 1 visual direction board

Academic figure visual direction board for a Knowledge-Based Systems journal paper about HMRS (Hierarchical Management Retrieval System), a self-hosted knowledge document management and tiered retrieval platform.

Create a clean editorial vector-style composition reference only, not the final scientific figure. White background, deep navy and blue-gray structure, restrained Okabe-Ito blue/green/amber/purple accents, no gradients, no 3D, no decorative technology clichés. Show three semantic layers on the left (Collection, Document, Segment), real service boundaries in the center (Browser/Web UI and Nuxt, Agent/CLI and MCP, FastAPI and MinerU), shared auditable state at the bottom (Markdown/assets, tree registry and per-KB YAML, Chroma/BGE-M3 plus BM25, Neo4j graph), and a catalogue-to-shelf-to-document-to-segment query ladder on the right. Use simple rounded rectangles, thin precise connectors, explicit optional gate styling, and balanced whitespace suitable for a two-column KBS paper. The board is for composition, hierarchy, and palette only. Do not rely on generated text, numbers, ports, logos, or exact labels; use abstract placeholder glyphs or blank label bars because all exact labels and arrows will be typeset later as editable vector text. No watermark, no fake citations, no invented modules.
