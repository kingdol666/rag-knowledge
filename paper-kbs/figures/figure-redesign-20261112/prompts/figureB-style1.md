# HMRS Figure B — Style 1 visual direction board

Academic figure visual direction board for a KBS paper showing a real knowledge-document lifecycle. Clean white editorial vector composition with two horizontal swimlanes. Upper lane: source files, parse and quality check, structure-aware split and enrichment, content-informed routing, save files and registries, entry-dependent index and post-check. Lower lane: question and scope, select pinned or all-library workflow, gather catalogue/BM25/vector/graph candidates, optional Laya segment judging, then three clearly separated outcomes: evidence plus sources, completed scoped not-found, and execution failure. A central shared-state ribbon connects the lanes. Use deep navy text, blue retrieval arrows, green ingestion arrows, amber routing/decision accents, violet shared-state band, grey failure branch, flat rounded rectangles, sparse precise arrows, no gradients or 3D. Composition reference only: exact labels, arrows, data semantics, and all text will be typeset later as native vectors. Do not invent automatic indexing, atomic writes, graph-as-evidence, or Laya-as-truth. No watermark or fake text.
