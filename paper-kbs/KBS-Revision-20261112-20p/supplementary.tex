\begin{figure*}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.78\textwidth]{fig6_margin_variants}
\caption{Two replays over the logged hybrid runs of this subsection; both
reuse recorded candidate pools and verdict scores rather than retrieving
again. (a)~A relative-margin replay: keep the top five plus every candidate
within $m$ of the pool's best score, $m$ swept 0.0--0.3; the solid curve
counts gold instances kept of the 17 that entered a pool, the grey curve the
mean candidates returned of the twelve submitted; dashed rules mark $m=0.15$
(16 of 17 golds kept) and $m=0.20$ (all 17). (b)~The verdict's four roles,
scored on identical candidates and identical verdict scores: fusion order,
verdict as re-ranker, verdict as filter, and rank fusion. Only the role
differs, which makes the panel an attribution rather than a benchmark; the
re-ranking role is the one that loses the gold.}
\label{fig:marginvariants}
\end{figure*}
