## 4. The length cap — the one substantive gap

The guide's cap is phrased in **double line spaced** pages and explicitly
includes tables and figures. Measured on this manuscript (2026-10-12):

| measure | value |
|---|---|
| body prose | ~16.2 k words |
| submission build (the flat package that would be uploaded) | 77 pages (12 pt, single spaced, single column, no subfolders) |
| preprint build | 76 pages (`paper-kbs/tex/main2.pdf`) |
| review copy (double spaced) | 111 pages (measured 2026-10-12, `paper-kbs/tex/review/main2.pdf`) |
| camera-ready preview (two column, `final,5p`) | 36 pages, 0 overfull (`paper-kbs/tex/journal/main2.pdf`) |
| supplementary material | 30 pages (`paper-kbs/supplementary/supplementary-material.pdf`, 16 campaigns + 16 configuration groups) |
| fig. + table environments | 9 figures, 21 table environments |
| KBS exemplars measured for comparison | bank-IRS (KBS 310, 2025) 19 pages / 10.3 k words; SurveyAgent-HKA (KBS 2026) 38 pages / 20.0 k words |

So the manuscript is roughly five times the *preferred* cap and the cap is
soft ("preferably"); the two papers KBS actually published alongside it are
themselves 1–2× over it. Three honest options, in the order I would take them:

1. **Submit as is** and let the editor decide. The cap is "preferably", the
   paper is a system description with the evidence that backs each claim, and
   it lands at ~35 two-column pages, i.e. inside the range of the exemplars.
2. **Move evidence to supplementary material** (`Appendix A. Supplementary
   data`) — **now done**: `paper-kbs/supplementary/supplementary-material.pdf`
   (**30 pages** after the 2026-10-12 repair pass; build command
   `pandoc supplementary-material.md -o supplementary-material.pdf --pdf-engine=pdflatex`)
   carries the complete inventory of every system-test campaign
   (A.1–A.16, adding the three-engine/three-channel cost campaign and the
   failure-mode catalogue provenance) and every measured retrieval configuration
   (B.1–B.16, adding the sixteen-question gold set and the per-question rank
   table's generator) with its artifact path, plus the limitations (Part C). The
   manuscript keeps the claims and their headline numbers; the supplement carries
   the full matrix.
   This is standard Elsevier practice and does not touch the claims.
   Note: the 13-page figure previously recorded here was not reproducible from the
   source with any default pandoc invocation.
3. **Cut to ~10–12 k words** by dropping one evidence group (the ablation
   section or the robustness section), which the earlier compression passes
   already identified as the only way to reach a 13 k target.

## 5. Artwork compliance

Audited with `paper-kbs/figures/_audit_artwork.py` (writes
`paper-kbs/tex/notes/artwork-audit.json`): all nine figures are **pure
vector, no raster xobjects, fonts embedded** (ArialMT / Arial-BoldMT /
Arial-ItalicMT, PDF fonttype 42), RGB, well under the size limits.

The defect found was **type size after reduction**, and it is now fixed. The
original audit measured effective minimum text at 3.8–5.4 pt against the
journal's 7 pt floor, because the source figures were exported with
`savefig.bbox="tight"`, so the PDF page was cropped to the ink and LaTeX then
scaled the artwork by an unknown factor (0.72–0.98). Each figure is now drawn
at exactly its placed width — 5.38 in full width, 2.69 in for Fig. 4, 3.34 in
for Fig. 7 — and `save()` pins the page to the designed canvas, so
`scale == 1.004` (the residual is the 388.9 pt measured `\textwidth` against
the 388.7 pt design width) and **every figure reports an effective minimum of
8.02–8.03 pt**, with all text set at 8.0 pt. Two asserts in `save()` keep it
that way: no text may leave the canvas, and the exported page must equal the
designed canvas. Re-check with
`D:\anaconda3\python.exe paper-kbs\figures\_audit_artwork.py`; the figures are
regenerated into `paper-kbs/figures/*.pdf|.png` (300 dpi) and copied to
`paper-kbs/tex/figs/`.

Multi-panel figures (Fig. 6, 8, 9) are the one stylistic rule the guide states
flatly ("do not submit multiple images or graphs combined into one file"),
with an exception for comparison panels. They are kept as comparison panels;
if the editor objects, each panel is already an independent drawing in
`paper-kbs/figures/figures.py`.
