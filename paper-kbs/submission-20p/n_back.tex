\section*{Data availability}
The platform source code, campaign logs and scripts, gold question sets,
replays and per-run JSON records cited in this paper are available at
\url{https://github.com/kingdol666/rag-knowledge} (benchmark suite:
\texttt{benchmark-suite/}; paper artifacts: \texttt{paper-kbs/}). The
evaluation inventory is indexed in \path{TEST-RESULTS/ARTIFACTS.md}. Within
\texttt{benchmark-suite/}: the end-to-end grid of Section~\ref{sec:e5} is
\path{results/runs/run-20261013T094350Z-5f9faac/}; original-annotation grades
are in \path{results/exp_r2_grade_original_revised.json}; corrected
annotations and their regrade are in
\path{data/papers/qa_questions_e5_corrected.json} and
\path{results/exp_r2_grade_corrected.json}; the grader is
\path{scripts/107_exp_grade.py}; and superseded campaigns stay in
\path{results/archive-superseded/}. Derived latency and cost statistics are in
\path{paper-kbs/tex/journal_20p/e5_numbers.json}. The corrected annotation set
is frozen for this submission (version ``2026-10-14 corrected evaluation
annotations'', frozen 2026-10-16, SHA-256
\texttt{86b07a9370984d42\ldots e62ece}), and the annotations, the
pre-correction question set and the grader are pinned together in
\path{paper-kbs/tex/journal_20p/.frozen-experiment-sha256.txt} so every
reported grade can be re-derived exactly. The supplementary material
(\path{paper-kbs/supplementary/supplementary-material.pdf}) carries the
campaign inventory, the appendix tables S1--S5 that
\ref{app:perq} cites, and the evaluation tables S6--S8. The 42-document industrial corpus is a demonstration
instance prepared for this study and is not customer data; it is not included
in the public repository, but the demonstration documents are available from
the authors on request. The headline ordering comparison does not depend on
its text: the judge-ordering row recomputes from the
released per-candidate scores in
\path{paper-kbs/experiments/results/exp1_retrieval.json}, the released gold
annotations and the grader, and the fusion lists behind the derived rows are
released for the anatomy query of Fig.~\ref{fig:anatomy} and retained per
question in the campaign records. An end-to-end re-run would additionally
require a substitute corpus; its construction recipe is documented with an
illustrative synthetic sample
(\path{paper-kbs/notes/industrial-corpus-substitute-recipe-2026-10-16.md}).

\section{Supplementary tables}
\label{app:perq}

Eight tables are carried in the supplementary material
(\path{paper-kbs/supplementary/supplementary-material.pdf}) rather than in
this build: Table~S1, the stores read by the three-source checks; S2, the
cross-lingual ingestion result; S3, the sixteen gold questions with their
design intent; S4, the per-question best-gold-rank grid; S5, the
lifecycle-operation outcomes, including the two that failed; and S6--S8, the
verdict-score threshold sweep and the two source-presence audits.
The per-question grid localizes the aggregate metrics: judge-score re-ranking
misses eight golds that remain in the fusion order, the derived survivor
replay restores those ranks but inherits Q16's upstream miss, and the Q14
candidate without a recorded score remains unclassified. The S1--S5 LaTeX
sources are archived under
\path{paper-kbs/tex/journal_20p/_appendix-archived/}; S6--S8 are generated by
the scripts named in the supplement.
