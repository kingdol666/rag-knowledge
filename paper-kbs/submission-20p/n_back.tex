(\path{paper-kbs/supplementary/supplementary-material.pdf}) carries the

twenty-document near-duplicate family sharing prefixes, vocabulary and
structure---is documented with an illustrative synthetic sample
(\path{paper-kbs/notes/industrial-corpus-substitute-recipe-2026-10-16.md}).

\section{Supplementary tables}
\label{app:perq}

Five tables are carried in the supplementary material
(\path{paper-kbs/supplementary/supplementary-material.pdf}) rather than in
this build, each beside the campaign that produced it: Table~S1, the stores
and fields read by the three-source consistency checks of
Section~\ref{sec:e2}; Table~S2, the cross-lingual ingestion result of
Section~\ref{sec:e1}, both rounds bit-for-bit identical; Table~S3, the sixteen
gold-annotated questions behind every retrieval number in
Section~\ref{sec:e4}, with their design intent; Table~S4, the per-question
best-gold-rank grid that decomposes Figure~\ref{fig:marginvariants}(b); and
Table~S5, the lifecycle-operation outcomes of Section~\ref{sec:e2}, one
operation per row, including the two that failed.
The per-question grid localizes the aggregate retrieval metrics: for the
industrial same-pool replay, judge-score re-ranking misses eight golds that
remain in the fusion order, while the derived survivor replay restores those
ranks for scored candidates but inherits Q16's upstream miss; the Q14
candidate without a recorded judge score remains unclassified. The LaTeX
sources of all five tables are archived in the repository under
\path{paper-kbs/tex/journal_20p/_appendix-archived/}.
