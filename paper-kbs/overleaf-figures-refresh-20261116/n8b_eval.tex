\subsection{E4: Operational knowledge and automated distillation}
\label{sec:e3}

The experience store holds operational lessons as retrievable
documents---addressed, indexed and searched as an ordinary document, with a
credibility tier deciding whether it may be shown at all
(Section~\ref{sec:org-experience}). Three case measurements exist: an
extraction campaign over one small knowledge base produced thirteen
candidate drafts (archived \path{experience_smoke.json} in the 2026-10-13
batch tarball; \texttt{extract\_candidates}~13, \texttt{drafts\_created}~13);
one end-to-end scenario was sedimented into one experience
(\texttt{exp-d6f4d47ae3d6}) that a later global experience search returned
first, at local similarity 0.711 (archived scenario A6 of the 2026-10-12
full-test campaign); and the draft-approval path was never exercised, because
the draft pool was empty (\texttt{count}~0) and \path{experience_draft_read}
and \path{experience_draft_approve} could not run; the heuristic extraction
path had run only in dry-run mode, returning a dump the quality gate would
reject. One scenario and thirteen candidates cannot support an effectiveness
claim; scheduled distillation, review and approval at scale, application
feedback and credibility decay went unexercised. One recorded lesson was
retrieved with its tier.

\subsection{E5: Content-layer configuration comparison}
\label{sec:ladder-opt}
A0 is the tiered baseline with route-card selection and a 40K reference-mode
judge. A0f retains route-card selection but reads full document windows inline
(up to 250K characters); A0h adds the top-eight vector-chunk windows to A0f,
with a 60K evidence cap. PD removes catalogue navigation and searches a
per-library top-six document union before full-window judging. A1b is a
scoped union of vector results from the ten evaluation libraries, merged by
score and judged in a 40K reference window; A2 uses a scoped vector union and
concatenated candidate snippets without the judge. A3 uses nominally name-only route cards, while read-all mechanically
scans every stored part for the target string. A1b and A2 use per-library
vector search, not an unrestricted all-library query. Route-card retrieval
uses top-30 vector candidates at cosine threshold 0.35 with a 0.30 retry.
These are recorded configuration details, not a single-factor design: A0h,
PD and A1b vary in routing, candidate construction and judging, and their
differences cannot be assigned to navigation alone. Some reference lookups
may use the gold domain; their frequency and impact are unknown. A3 may
retrieve outside the evaluation scope. The frozen traces do not preserve
complete prompts and configuration hashes for every historical arm, which
limits exact reconstruction.

In the 11 November aggregate, gold strings were contained for A0/A0f/A0h/PD/
A1b/A2/A3/read-all in 37/45/52/46/39/25/33/72 of 72 questions, respectively
(Figure~\ref{fig:ladder}); these are evidence-string proxies, not answer
correctness. Supplementary Tables S12--S14 give the per-arm intervals and
paired records. The A0h Wilson 95\% interval is [0.610, 0.812]. In exploratory,
unadjusted paired records, A0h contained the string while A1b did not on 13
questions and the reverse occurred on none (two-sided exact McNemar
$p=0.00024$). PD led A1b on seven questions with no reverse
($p=0.0156$). A0h led PD on seven questions while PD led on one
($p=0.0703$). A2 contained the string for 25/72 against 37/72 for A0
(uncorrected exact McNemar $p=0.0227$; the later record repeats 25/72). A2 and
A0 also differ in routing, candidate construction and returned text, so this
comparison does not isolate the gate. These records do not show a statistically resolved advantage
over PD and are not unbiased deployment estimates: A0, A1b and A3 can use
the gold domain to resolve judge references, with unknown frequency and impact.
In a separate 40-question diagnostic, the gold string appeared in the
returned text for 24/40 BM25 cases and 19/40 dense-search cases. Concatenating
the top five texts from each list gave 26/40; despite the historical label
\texttt{F-rrf}, this operation did not compute reciprocal-rank fusion. BM25
reads the local corpus, whereas dense search uses the global index. These
substring checks use a 40-question subset and different scopes and returned-text
construction from the 72-question evidence-pack experiment. They do not measure
gold-document Hit@5; no paired comparison is reported across the two tests.

A later same-question record yields 39/44/51/45/39/25 for the first six arms,
respectively. Per-question agreement with the frozen record is 94.4\% for A0,
98.6\% for A0f, A0h and PD, and 100\% for A1b and A2; the same question set
is reused, but the follow-up corpus contains 906 parts versus 879 in the frozen
record, and complete execution provenance is unavailable. In a separately
added later A3 record, containment is 25/72, the scripted gold domain is
selected for 49/72, with 13 missing top candidates and 23 empty packs; their
causes are not established; the historical records do not isolate the role of
route-card indexing state. Later read-all is
incomplete. The frozen read-all is a mechanical substring scan, not an agent
that reads and answers. Retrieval thresholds follow platform defaults (score 0.35 with a 0.30
retry, top-30 wide net).
A0h recorded 309 KB and 13.8 s per question versus
24.3 MB and 253.7 s for that scan; the recorded-byte definitions differ and
may include overlap.

In the latest R3 content-layer record, A0h had the highest
observed exact-string containment among the completed R3 arms on this corpus
and question set (51/72, 70.8\%). PD, A0f, A0/A1b and A2 contained the string
in 45/72, 44/72, 39/72 and 25/72 cases, respectively. The paired A0h--A0
contrast was 12--0 (exact two-sided $p=0.00049$); this is an exploratory,
unadjusted proxy comparison, not a semantic-answer or single-factor hierarchy
test. R3 A3 was 25/72 with scripted gold-domain routing 49/72, and is reported
as a separate observation because the cause of its change is unresolved.
R3 read-all was incomplete at the report cutoff (3 completed gold
rows), so the frozen 72/72 read-all result remains the mechanical upper-bound
reference rather than a latest-run comparison.

Among A0's 35 misses, A0f, A0h and PD contain the string in 8, 15 and 9 cases,
respectively. These sets overlap and are not additive. A post-hoc search
classified five first matching string locations outside a nominal window and
30 inside; the chosen matching file need not be the gold document or a
candidate actually read, so these categories do not identify the causes of
misses (Figure~\ref{fig:miss-overlap}).
