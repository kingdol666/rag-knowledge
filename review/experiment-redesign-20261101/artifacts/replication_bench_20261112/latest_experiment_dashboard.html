Read-all is an upper-bound diagnostic

Frozen read-all: 72/72 gold strings, 64,584 calls total, 897 calls/question, 24.3 MB/question, 253.7 s/question. It is a mechanical substring scan over 879 parts and generated no answers.

R3 read-all: only 3 completed rows at the time of the frozen R3 report, all 3/3 hits, with the corpus expanded to 906 parts and about 25.5 MB/question. It must not replace the frozen read-all value until a complete run exists.

What the evidence supports

  • Supported: A0h's latest complete R3 containment is highest among the complete tested content-layer arms, and its advantage over R3 A0 is statistically detectable in an exploratory paired test.
  • Supported: A0h, PD and A0f reproduce their ordering and remain within 1.4 percentage points of frozen values.
  • Not supported: a semantic answer-accuracy claim, a causal hierarchy claim isolated from all other components, or a comparison of A0h against a completed latest read-all run.
  • A3 caveat: name-only route-card containment is numerically sensitive to route-card index health; use the stable qualitative conclusion that removing descriptions makes routing unreliable, not one A3 percentage as a universal constant.
