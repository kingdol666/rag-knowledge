Key Findings

  • MoE KB-routing achieves 6.3× lower cross-domain false-positive rate (8.7% vs 54-62% for flat baselines) — the MoE gate's primary value is domain precision, not raw recall.
  • Hybrid RRF fusion delivers the best raw recall (85.9% Recall@5) by combining BM25's lexical precision with dense semantic matching.
  • Content verification is the single most critical QDCVR component (ΔP@5=-0.152 when removed), more impactful than domain scoping or query rewriting.
  • MoE routing accuracy is 71.7% (33/46) — vocabulary-overlap routing; embedding-based routing would further improve KB selection.
  • Optimal hyperparameters: content_threshold=6, score_threshold=0.35, stage1_top_k=20 — all at the robust flat region of their sensitivity curves.
  • QDCVR's design targets precision over recall — ideal for domain-isolated knowledge bases where cross-domain contamination degrades answer quality.
