Key Findings

MoE KB-routing achieves 1.6× lower cross-domain FPR (34.8% vs 54.3% Hybrid-RRF) — the primary value of QDCVR is domain precision, not raw recall
Hybrid RRF fusion delivers best raw recall at 78.3% by combining BM25 lexical precision with BGE-M3 dense semantic matching
QDCVR-MoE achieves highest P@1 (45.7%) and MRR (0.477) — when MoE correctly routes the KB, the top result is most likely correct
Content verification is the most critical QDCVR component: ΔP@5=−0.152 (58% of total quality gain); domain scoping is second (ΔP@5=−0.078, ΔFPR=+0.275)
MoE routing accuracy: 62% — vocabulary-overlap routing; upgrading to embedding-based routing predicts further improvement
Optimal hyperparameters: content_threshold=6, score_threshold=0.35, stage1_top_k=20 — all at robust plateau regions of sensitivity curves
